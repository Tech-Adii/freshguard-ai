What We Are Doing (The Core Problem)

Large Language Models (LLMs) suffer from a fundamental, critical vulnerability: Temporal Blindness. An LLM's knowledge cutoff permanently freezes its understanding of reality at the date it finished pre-training.

When users query dynamic, fast-changing facts — such as model pricing, software APIs, corporate leadership, or regulations — standard LLMs generate obsolete information with 100% confidence, presenting historical 2022-2024 states as active 2026 facts.

FreshGuard AI solves this by acting as an autonomous temporal fact-checker. It intercepts AI-generated responses, deconstructs them into atomic assertions, measures temporal volatility, cross-references live 2026 ground-truth web evidence, and dynamically rewrites outdated assertions with verified reality.

Why It Is Used (Business & Technical Impact)
Financial & Cost Protection

Prevents AI systems from quoting outdated pricing (e.g. legacy GPT-4 $30/1M vs modern GPT-4o $2.50/1M), saving enterprises from billing disputes and miscalculated cost models.

Developer & API Reliability

Flags deprecated software methods and frameworks (e.g. Next.js Pages router vs modern App Router), eliminating broken code and endless debugging loops.

Brand & Legal Compliance

Guards against hallucinations involving executive leadership turnover (e.g. Parag Agrawal vs Elon Musk) and rapidly shifting legal/compliance mandates.

Automated Truth Rewriting

Unlike traditional detectors that simply flag an error, FreshGuard automatically generates the verified 2026 ground-truth replacement ready for production use.

System Architecture & Technologies Used
1
Atomic Claim Extraction & Temporal Volatility:

Deconstructs text into discrete factual assertions and calculates a Volatility Score ($0.0 - 1.0$) based on category decay rates.

2
Live 2026 Evidence Retrieval (Tavily Search API):

Executes targeted temporal search queries across live web consensus to collect authoritative URLs, timestamps, and quotes.

3
Contradiction Reasoning (Bifrost Gateway / Gemini 3.5):

Cross-examines claim assertions against retrieved evidence to classify status: Fresh, Stale, Evergreen, or Unverified.

4
Scoring, Visual Diffing & Audio Voiceover:

Calculates percentage Freshness Score ($0-100\%$), renders side-by-side strikethrough diffs, and synthesizes audio briefings.
